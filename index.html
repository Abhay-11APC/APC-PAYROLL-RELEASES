APC PAYROLL

A Windows desktop app made by Ajay Planners & Consultants for its own office staff. It is used to prepare monthly payroll and labour-law records — EPF, ESI, attendance and registers — for the establishments the firm serves.

APC PAYROLL is not sold or offered to the public. Only people given an account by the firm can sign in.

How APC PAYROLL uses Google Drive

  • The firm connects one office Google account, once.
  • The app saves its own backup files and a monthly audit file in that Drive.
  • It can only see files it made itself — never your other Drive files.
  • It never deletes anything in Drive, and nothing is sold or shared.
