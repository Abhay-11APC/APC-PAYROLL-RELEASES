Privacy policy — APC PAYROLL

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who we are

APC PAYROLL is a Windows desktop app made by Ajay Planners & Consultants for its own office staff, to prepare payroll and labour-law records for the establishments the firm serves. It is not offered to the public.

What the app keeps

Payroll, attendance and employee records of the firm’s client establishments, and a record of who did what in the app. These are kept on the firm’s office PCs and in the firm’s private cloud database. Each staff member sees only the establishments assigned to them.

Google Drive

The firm connects one office Google account, once. APC PAYROLL uses only the Google permission “see, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app” (drive.file). It saves its own backup files and a monthly audit Excel file in folders it creates. It cannot see or open any other file in that Drive, and it does not delete files.

The Google sign-in key

The key Google gives after sign-in is kept in a protected store in the firm’s private cloud. It is never stored on staff PCs.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any of this information. It is not used for advertising. Information from Google is used only to save and read the app’s own files in the firm’s Drive.

Google API Services

APC PAYROLL’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The Super Admin can disconnect Google Drive in the app at any time. Access can also be removed from the Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already saved in Drive stay there until the account owner deletes them.
